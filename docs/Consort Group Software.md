**Overall Shipment Flow:**

Origin & Booking Phase

Step 01 – Order Lock

* Customer confirms the shipment.  
* Shipping order is created and locked for execution.

Step 02 – SWIFT / LC Advice

* Bank issues the financial confirmation (LC/SWIFT advice).  
* Shipment becomes financially authorized.

Step 03 – Booking Confirmation

* Shipping line booking is confirmed.  
* Container allocation and vessel planning begin.

Step 04 – Documentation & Pre-Arrival Preparation

* Required shipping documents are prepared.  
* Port and logistics teams are notified for execution.

---

### Inland Transportation Phase

Step 05 – Container Release Order (CRO)

* Payments are verified.  
* Shipping line releases the Container Release Order (CRO), allowing pickup of the empty container.

Step 06 – Cargo Pickup

* Consort collects cargo from the shipper.  
* Cargo is transported to the staging terminal through monitored transport routes.

---

### Port & Customs Phase

Step 07 – Customs Declaration

* Goods Declaration (GD) is submitted electronically through the national customs system after arrival at the port.

Step 08 – Customs Inspection & Container Sealing

* Customs officers inspect the cargo.  
* Container is sealed using an authorized tamper-proof seal.

Step 09 – Terminal Handover

* The sealed container is handed over to the terminal.  
* It is assigned a vessel loading position.

---

### Ocean Freight & Financial Settlement Phase

Step 10 – Bill of Lading (BOL) Issuance

* After loading the container onto the vessel, the shipping line issues the Master Bill of Lading, confirming shipment and ownership.

Step 11 – Document Submission to Bank

* Consort submits the original BOL and shipping documents to the negotiating bank to satisfy LC requirements.

Step 12 – Telex Release

* Before vessel arrival, the destination bank verifies the documents.  
* A Telex Release is issued to authorize cargo delivery.

Step 13 – Cargo Inspection & Acceptance

* Buyer inspects the cargo at the destination terminal.  
* Cargo is checked against contractual and quality requirements.

Step 14 – Final Payment & Title Transfer

* Upon buyer acceptance:  
* Escrow releases payment to the seller.  
* Ownership of the cargo is officially transferred to the buyer.
